How to use this
  1. Pick your logo. Click Choose a logo (or drag a PNG or JPG onto the box). Nothing gets uploaded anywhere. Everything happens right here in your browser.
  2. Get rid of the background. The tool guesses the background color from the edges of your picture. If it guessed wrong, click on the background in the Before picture to pick it yourself. The gray-and-white checkerboard in the After picture means "see-through."
  3. Adjust the Tolerance slider. Slide it right if bits of the old background are still showing around the edges. Slide it left if parts of the logo are disappearing. Logos saved as JPG usually need a bit more.
  4. Holes in letters? If the background still shows inside letters like O, A, or D, check Also remove this color inside the logo. Leave it off if your logo uses the same color on purpose, like white letters on a white-background logo.
  5. Add the sticker outline. The first outline hugs the logo (white by default). The outer outline goes around that (black by default). Change the thickness with the sliders, and pick any color or tap a team color. Uncheck either one to turn it off.
  6. Check how it looks. The previews at the bottom show your logo on a light background, a dark background, and on each of the team colors.
  7. Download. Set the Padding (empty space around the logo), then click a size button. For any other size, type a width and height and click Download custom size. You get a PNG with a see-through background, with the logo centered.
